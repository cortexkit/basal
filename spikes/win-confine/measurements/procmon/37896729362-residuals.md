Each `result (count)` cell counts probes that returned that result. Win32/NTSTATUS 0 means success; Win32 5 and NTSTATUS 0xc0000022 mean ACCESS DENIED; Win32 1655 means ERROR_DYNAMIC_CODE_BLOCKED.
